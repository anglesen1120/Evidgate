## Accepted design decisions

Reviewer-supported choices already expressed by the proposal; **not new owner approvals or waivers for the findings**:

- Keep the hierarchy as native nested lists with separate disclosure buttons and selection links. Do not introduce a partial ARIA tree merely to copy the shared fixture's `treeitem` handler. Optional directional shortcuts need their own implementation evidence.
- Below 768px retain inspectable project/context/history/credit information and disable/remove only mutations with explanation; do not replace the page with a blank mobile notice. The resize handoff and open-confirmation cancellation remain mandatory.
- Preserve immutable version history, explicit restore-as-new and one atomic pending-batch apply. A local success card is an illustration, not persistence evidence.
- Keep pending review separate from approval; preserve dirty drafts/queues on conflict and require explicit reload/retry. No silent merge, auto-apply or invented provider fallback.
- Keep confirmed zero distinct from unavailable, show organization-scoped credit facts, and block only the AI call when authorized estimate/balance is unavailable. Manual authoring remains available subject to its own grant.
- Keep source/provenance/user text as escaped plain text and fixture IDs/tariffs visibly illustrative. The contract and explicit design deltas override unsuitable reference interactions; sidebar-only shared generation does not relax the baked-HTML workspace requirement.
- Keep interface locale separate from generated-output languages. Apply the actual Unicode code-point contract to guidance limits, not JavaScript UTF-16 length.
