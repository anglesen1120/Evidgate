## 4. Counsel brief (Vietnamese data-protection lawyer)

Send these as one engagement during Stage B. Each answer goes into `docs/compliance/` with the counsel's name and date (11.8.1 `manifest.yaml`). The plans assert no answer.

1. **Baseline text.** Do the article numbers we cite from the LuatVietnam English translation (Arts. 2, 8, 9, 20, 21, 23, 37) match the official Vietnamese text of Law 91/2025/QH15? What is its effective date, and are there transition rules for a service that starts processing in 2026–2027? (OI-11-01)
2. **Implementing decree.** Is Decree 356/2025/ND-CP ⚠️ the implementing decree of the PDPL? What are its date, effective date and the articles that bind a small SaaS processor (dossier forms, filing channel, DPO duty, notice forms, exemptions for small or start-up businesses)? (OI-11-01, OI-11-05, OI-11-08)
3. **Roles.** Under the Art. 2 definitions, is Evidgate a processor for customer test artifacts (screenshots, DOM, traces, logs) and a controller for its own account and login data? Does the Law recognise a mixed controller-processor role, and what follows from it? Does consent sit only with the customer? (OI-11-02)
4. **DPA content and form.** What minimum contents does Art. 37.2 (and the decree) require in a processing contract? Is a bilingual e-signed or click-through DPA valid? Must the Vietnamese version prevail? (OI-11-03)
5. **Pilot timing.** If a pilot sends only redacted test results with "no regulated personal data" (O1 §2), is a DPA still required before the first upload? Is a short data-handling note enough before the DPA is signed? (OI-11-03, OI-11-04)
6. **Personal-data scope.** Are these personal data under the PDPL: (a) client IP addresses held in memory as rate-limit keys, (b) a pseudonymous UUID `subject_id` kept in audit rows after the person's record is erased, (c) org ids and hashes in audit checkpoints, (d) redacted test results? (OI-11-04)
7. **Cross-border transfer triggers.** Under Art. 20.1, which of these is a transfer: storage with a Vietnam-region provider owned from abroad, an offshore backup or replica, an offshore audit-checkpoint bucket, sending healing context to an LLM provider abroad, administration by the operator from outside Vietnam? (OI-11-05, OI-10-01)
8. **Who files and when.** When a processor stores data abroad on the controller's instruction, who is the "transferring party" that files the Art. 20.2 transfer impact assessment within 60 days? What triggers the clock? Is one dossier per provider enough, or is one needed per customer? Same questions for the LLM provider. (OI-11-05, OI-11-21)
9. **Processing impact dossier.** Under Art. 21.1/21.3, what must the processor prepare and keep versus file, and can our customer-facing template serve the controller's filing? (OI-11-05)
10. **Data-subject requests.** Which rights apply (access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability)? What are the response deadline and extension rules, and does the clock start when the subject asks the controller or when the request reaches the processor? Must a processor forward requests, and within what time? How should identity be verified? (OI-11-06)
11. **Request records.** How long must the request record and its correspondence be kept, and may the processor keep it at all? (OI-11-07)
12. **Breach.** Art. 23.1 sets 72 h. Whose clock is that, and does the Law or decree set a separate processor→controller deadline or notice contents? Is 4 h from triage confirmation defensible? (OI-11-08)
13. **Sub-processors.** Does the Law or decree require prior specific or general authorisation for sub-processors? Is a 30-day objection window with a termination right adequate? (OI-11-09)
14. **Retention and deletion.** Are there statutory minimum or maximum periods for security/access logs, audit trails, backups, or deletion completion? Does a backup that expires within 30 days satisfy a deletion request? (OI-11-10, OI-11-11)
15. **Exposure.** Do the fine bands (repo cites Art. 8.3–8.5) apply per violation or per year, and does the decree reduce them for small businesses? (OI-11-01; context for the O3 decision in OI-10-01)
