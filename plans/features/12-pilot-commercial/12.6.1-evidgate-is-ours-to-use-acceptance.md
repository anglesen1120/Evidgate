## 1. Goal and non-goals

**Goal.** Make the "Evidgate" mark legally usable: a documented clearance search in Nice classes 9 and 42 (Vietnam first, WIPO/US registers watched), a NOIP application filed before public launch, the Madrid/US decision recorded with real fee numbers, and domain/handle consistency secured inside a fixed budget.

**Non-goals.** Brand/design work; enforcing or opposing third-party marks (route to counsel if the search surfaces a conflict); patent or copyright matters; the public launch decision itself (00 §4 gate — this story only supplies the O7 evidence).

## 2. User stories and acceptance criteria

- As the owner, I get a search result with counsel's risk opinion before any money is spent on launch branding, so a rename happens while it's still cheap (area plan §5 collision case).
- As the founder, I hold a NOIP application number before public launch and a recorded decision on international protection with the verified costs.

| # | Acceptance criterion (testable) |
|---|---|
| AC1 | `docs/brand/trademark-search-report.md` covers "Evidgate" word mark in Nice classes 9 and 42 on the VN (NOIP) register plus WIPO Global Brand DB and USPTO TESS watch results, with search date, exact terms used, and counsel's written risk opinion attached |
| AC2 | `docs/brand/vn-application.md` records the NOIP application number, filing date, classes 9+42, the mark representation used, and the actual costs (official fee + attorney) — the filing date precedes the public-launch gate |
| AC3 | `docs/brand/international-decision.md` records the O7 decision (Madrid designation set and/or US filing vs defer) with the budget built only from verified fees: WIPO 653/903 CHF basic + 100 CHF/member + 100 CHF/class-beyond-3 ✅, USPTO $350/class (+$200 free-form, +$150 statement of use) ✅; VN-side figures are marked ⚠️ until quoted |
| AC4 | `docs/brand/handles.md` lists the domain/handle inventory (primary domain, key TLDs, GitHub/npm/NuGet org names) with status `held|defensive|unavailable`, and total defensive spend stays inside the budget recorded in the file |

## 7. Jobs and integrations

- Attorney engagement for search + filing (counsel chosen per O7/D8).

- Feeds: the O7 decision record → DECISIONS §10 change log; a collision finding → rename decision before launch branding spend (blocks marketing asset work implicitly via the launch gate).
